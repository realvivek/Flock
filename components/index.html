Pole, mount and field of view

Flock’s standard installation is a dedicated breakaway pole set in soil by a Flock crew. The camera is band-clamped at about 10 feet and aimed diagonally along the lane so that the rear plates of departing vehicles pass through the field of view.

Power and network connections

The Falcon has no network cable. It runs on solar power or on a pole’s electricity and sends its reads over a cellular connection. Network cables and fiber appear in one setup, the Wing gateway, which takes video from cameras an agency already owns.
