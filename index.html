Pole and mount

Pole, mount and field of view

Flock's standard installation is a dedicated breakaway pole set in soil by a Flock crew. The camera is band-clamped at about ten feet and aimed diagonally along the lane so that the rear plates of departing vehicles pass through the field of view.

Power and cable

Power and network connections

The Falcon has no wired network interface and is not a Power over Ethernet device. Fiber, SFP modules and PoE are used in one configuration, the Wing gateway, which ingests video from an agency's existing cameras.

Data path · capture to deletion

Data path in twelve stages

A vehicle triggers the motion sensor, the image sensor captures six to twelve frames, the on-device model reads the plate and classifies the vehicle, and the result is transmitted over LTE. The processing location is stated at each stage.
