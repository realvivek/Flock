Pole and mount

Pole, mount and field of view

Flock's standard installation is a dedicated breakaway pole set in soil by a Flock crew. The camera is band-clamped at about ten feet and aimed diagonally along the lane so that the rear plates of departing vehicles pass through the field of view. No conductor runs to grade: power is supplied by the pole-top solar array and data is transmitted over LTE.

Drag the camera or use the sliders. The footprint drawn on the road uses the field of view from Flock's specification sheet.

Power and cable

Power and network connections

The Falcon has no wired network interface and is not a Power over Ethernet device. In the solar configuration the conductors on the pole carry DC from the solar array and the external battery box to the camera's rear connector. In the AC configuration a licensed electrician terminates 120 V mains in a junction box and Flock's AC kit converts it to DC.

Fiber, SFP modules and PoE are used in one configuration: Flock Wing, a gateway appliance installed in a network closet that ingests video from an agency's existing cameras and runs plate recognition on it. That configuration is modelled as the third path.

Data path in twelve stages
